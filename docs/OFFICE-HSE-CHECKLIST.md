# Office HSE risk catalogue and optional coaching modules

This catalogue separates screen-related ergonomics from broader occupational safety. It is an educational checklist, not a completed site-specific risk assessment.

## A. Display screen equipment (DSE) ergonomics
| Topic | Check | Possible Ergonomy intervention |
|---|---|---|
| Monitor | In front; readable without leaning; top at or slightly below eye height | One-time guided setup |
| Viewing distance | Generally comfortable around 50-100cm (OSHA), adjust for screen size and vision | Setup card; not a rigid universal distance |
| Reflection/glare | No direct sunlight or ceiling lamp reflections | Optional morning checklist |
| Chair/back | Seat height and lumbar support adjusted | Setup card |
| Legs/feet | Feet on ground or support; no pressure behind knees | Setup card |
| Keyboard/mouse | Elbows close; wrists neutral; input devices within reach | Setup card |
| Laptop | External keyboard/mouse and raised display for prolonged use | Laptop-specific onboarding |
| Posture | Avoid fixed position for long periods | Configurable change-posture reminder |
| Visual workload | Regular gaze refocusing/blinking and time away from screen | Eye reminder |
| Screen workload | Schedule shorter, more frequent breaks/change of tasks | Move/change-activity reminder |

## B. Other office HSE topics (non-periodic or context-aware)
| Topic | Suggested action | Reminder policy |
|---|---|---|
| Trailing cables and clutter | Keep walking routes free; arrange power leads safely | Onboarding / monthly self-check, not frequent popups |
| Damaged cables/sockets | Report defective electrical items promptly; avoid unsafe repairs | Safety information card, never automatic fault detection |
| Fire and emergency routes | Know workplace exits and site procedures; never obstruct exits | Workplace-specific onboarding |
| Manual handling | Assess risk and avoid hazardous lifts where practicable | Contextual guidance; no one-size-fits-all weight threshold |
| Thermal comfort/ventilation | Report poor airflow or unreasonable indoor conditions | Optional self-check; no universal thermostat setpoint |
| Lighting | Suitable task lighting; control glare and reflections | Setup card |
| Drinking water | Ensure access; individual hydration needs differ | Optional non-medical nudge |
| Noise/distractions | Report problematic noise; configure quiet focus times | User preference / organization assessment |
| Psychosocial workload | Encourage manageable work, breaks, role clarity and reporting | Respectful wellbeing education, never surveillance |
| Accessibility | Allow individualized settings, colors, controls, font size, and low-motion mode | Product accessibility requirements |
| Eye symptoms or persistent musculoskeletal pain | Raise through occupational-health/clinical channels | Information and referral, not diagnosis |

## C. Product rules

1. No fixed mandatory posture angle, fluid intake target, or forced hourly movement for all staff.
2. Workplace risk assessment and equipment changes remain the employer's responsibility.
3. Pause/delay in lock, idle, call and presentation contexts where feasible. First MVP covers lock and idle only.
4. No employee-level productivity monitoring, webcam inference, screenshots or keystroke logging.
5. Distinguish an informational nudge from an emergency safety warning.
6. Never present completion of a reminder as proof of HSE compliance.
7. Incorporate workplace accessibility requirements and individual reasonable adjustments.
8. For country-specific deployment, obtain local HSE/legal review rather than treating UK/US guidance as local law.
